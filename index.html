The Rise of Social Machines — Further Reading

Companion page for the Communications of the ACM Viewpoint by Emilio Ferrara, marking ten years since "The Rise of Social Bots" (CACM 59(7), 2016).

The Viewpoint names several detection tools and generation techniques without formal citation, to stay inside CACM's reference limit for the format. This page is the entry point those references would have been: one citation per technique, for readers who want to go further.

Account-level bot detection

Davis, Varol, Ferrara, Flammini & Menczer — BotOrNot: A System to Evaluate Social Bots (2016)
The tool later renamed Botometer. Supervised classification over content, network, temporal, and metadata signals; the first public web service for account-level bot scoring.
doi.org/10.1145/2872518.2889302
Yang, Ferrara & Menczer — Botometer 101: Social Bot Practicum for Computational Social Scientists (2022)
A practitioner's guide to the current version of Botometer: what its scores mean, where they fail, and how to use them responsibly in research.
doi.org/10.1007/s42001-022-00177-5

Coordination and the field's methodological shift

Cresci — A Decade of Social Bot Detection (2020)
A field-wide retrospective covering the same decade as the CACM Viewpoint, from account-level classification to coordination detection, with a taxonomy of what each generation of methods could and could not see.
doi.org/10.1145/3409116

Synthetic faces (GAN-generated identities)

Goodfellow et al. — Generative Adversarial Nets (2014)
The foundational paper introducing generative adversarial networks (GANs), the technique behind the synthetic profile photos discussed in the piece.
papers.nips.cc
Karras, Laine & Aila — A Style-Based Generator Architecture for GANs (StyleGAN, 2019)
The architecture behind the high-resolution, photorealistic synthetic faces that made GAN-generated profile photos hard to distinguish from real ones by 2019.
openaccess.thecvf.com

AI-generated text detection

Gehrmann, Strobelt & Rush — GLTR: Statistical Detection and Visualization of Generated Text (2019)
An early tool for spotting machine-generated text from its statistical fingerprint, from the pre-LLM era when detection was still tractable at the sentence level.
aclanthology.org/P19-3019

Cited in the Viewpoint

Minici, Cinus, Luceri & Ferrara — Uncovering Coordinated Cross-Platform Information Operations (2024)
Source of Figure 2(a)-(d) in the Viewpoint: the coordinated-account and mock-news-site artifacts from a real 2024 network, detected from co-sharing behavior alone.
doi.org/10.5210/fm.v29i11.13831
dotNex — Influence Operations on TikTok During the Iran War (2026)
Source of Figure 2(e)-(k): the AI-generated soldier personas and their shared production-pipeline signature.
reports.dotnex.ai
